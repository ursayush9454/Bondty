Feeling lonely? Chat with a listener in India

You can feel alone even in a crowded hostel, office or city. If there is no one to talk to tonight, a listener on Bondty can hear you out over chat or a voice call.

Loneliness is more common than you think

Moving to a new city, starting a job, living away from family or simply a long night can all bring on loneliness. It is a normal human feeling, and it helps to say it out loud to someone who will not judge you.

Akelapan dur kare: small steps to try

  • Message or call one old friend today.
  • Step outside for a short walk.
  • Join a hobby group, class or community.
  • When there is no one around, talk to a listener.

For more ideas read 7 simple things to do when you feel lonely.

How a Bondty listener can help

Pick a verified listener by rating, language and topic, then start a chat or voice call. Your number stays hidden, listeners see only your first name, and you pay per minute from a prepaid wallet. A listener is not a replacement for friends, family or professional care, but being heard can make a hard moment feel lighter.

Feeling lonely in a new city?

Moving to Delhi, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune or any new city for studies or a job often means leaving friends and family behind. The first few weeks can be the loneliest, especially at night. Talking to a listener will not replace friends, but it can give you someone to talk to while you build a new routine.

Common questions

Can talking to a listener really help when I feel alone?

Being heard can make a hard moment feel lighter for many people. If loneliness lasts for weeks or affects your sleep, appetite or daily life, please also speak to a doctor or counsellor.
