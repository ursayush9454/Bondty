FAQ

About payments and conversations.

How am I charged?

Each listener’s rate is shown in coins per minute. The timer starts when the conversation starts. A partial minute counts as a full minute. 1 coin = ₹1.

What if the listener doesn’t accept my request?

You aren’t charged. Nothing leaves your wallet until the conversation starts.

What if my balance runs out mid-session?

The session ends automatically. You get a warning when balance is low, and you can top up to keep talking.

Can the listener see my number?

No. Listeners only see your first name, and you can use any name you like instead of your real one.

Is Bondty free to download?

Yes. Downloading and browsing are free. You pay only for conversation time from a prepaid wallet.

Is Bondty safe?

Bondty is built safety first. Numbers and links are hidden in chat, listeners see only your first name, every partner goes through ID and profile verification, and you can report any session in one tap. The platform is for adults aged 18 and over. Read more on the safety page.

Are the listeners therapists or doctors?

Bondty is a conversation platform, not a medical, therapy or emergency service. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees. For medical or mental health care, please see a qualified doctor or therapist, and in an emergency contact your local emergency services.

How do I recharge my wallet with UPI?

Log in with your mobile number and OTP, then top up your wallet. You can pay with UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay. 1 coin = ₹1, and some packs add bonus coins, shown in the app before you pay. See pricing for details.

How do I get a refund?

If money was debited but coins were not added, or you were charged twice, the amount is refunded to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days. Unused purchased coins can be refunded if you ask within 7 days of the recharge by emailing hello@deificdigital.com or support@bondty.com or using Help and Support in the app. Bonus coins are not refundable. Full details are in the Refund and Cancellation policy.

Who can use Bondty?

Bondty is for adults aged 18 and over: college students, working professionals and anyone who wants someone to talk to. If you are under 18, please talk to a parent, teacher or school counsellor instead.

Is Bondty a dating app?

No. Bondty is not a dating service. It is a platform for conversation with verified listeners, and it does not provide medical or therapy services either.

Can I talk in Hindi or Hinglish?

Each listener's profile shows the languages they speak, along with ratings, topics and the per-minute rate. Check the profile before you start so you can pick a listener who speaks the way you do.

What can I talk about on Bondty?

Anything on your mind: exam stress, work pressure, loneliness, family talks, interview tips or just venting after a long day. Listeners are there to listen without judgement. See talk to someone online for more.

How can I become a listener?

Get the Bondty Partner app, complete ID and profile verification, go online and accept chat, voice or video requests. You earn for every minute you help someone and can withdraw your earnings from the app. See the partner page.

Download Bondty and talk to someone who listens.
