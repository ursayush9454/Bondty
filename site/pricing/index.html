Pricing

All prices in Indian Rupees (₹), inclusive of applicable taxes

Bondty is a paid listener app for India that is free to download and free to browse. You pay only for the conversation time you use, from a prepaid wallet. 1 coin = ₹0.80.

Wallet recharge

1 coin = ₹0.80, so every rupee you add becomes coins at that rate. Bigger recharges also add bonus coins on top, so you get more minutes for the same money.
