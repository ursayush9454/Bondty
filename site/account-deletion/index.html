Account Deletion

Bondty

ℹ️ This legal text is available in English only.

App: Bondty User App and Bondty Partner App · Developer: Deific Digital

You can ask us to delete your Bondty account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This applies to both Users and Partners. Choose whichever option is easier for you.

Option 1: Delete from the app

  1. Open the Bondty User App or Bondty Partner App and log in.
  2. Go to your account or profile settings.
  3. Select the account deletion option and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion from this page

Use this if you cannot open the app or have already uninstalled it. Fill in the details below. We will use your registered mobile number and account type to verify that the account is yours.

The button opens your email app with a ready message addressed to support@bondty.com. Send it from any email address. You can also write to us directly at that address, or at hello@deificdigital.com, with your registered mobile number and account type.

What happens next

  1. We verify the request using your registered mobile number and account type.
  2. After verification, we delete your account and the personal data linked to it.
  3. We confirm with you once the deletion is complete.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Account details (mobile number, name or display name, gender, profile details)Deleted or anonymised
Partner profile (photo, bio, topics, interests, languages)Deleted or anonymised
Partner ID documents and bank or UPI payout detailsDeleted, except where the law requires us to keep a record
Text chat messages and session historyDeleted or anonymised, subject to legal and safety requirements
Transaction, payout, tax and accounting recordsKept for the period required by law
Records needed for fraud prevention, security or a legal disputeKept only as long as required for that purpose

Bondty does not record voice or video calls, so there are no call recordings to delete. Temporary suspension or deactivation of an account is not the same as deletion.

Before you delete

  • Deleted accounts cannot be restored.
  • Check your wallet balance. Partners should also withdraw any eligible earnings first.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or data.

For how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy.
