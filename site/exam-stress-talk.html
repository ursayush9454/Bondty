Exam stress? Talk it out with someone who listens

Exam season can make everything feel heavy: the syllabus, the results, what family expects. Talking does not replace studying, but saying your worries out loud often makes the pressure feel lighter. Exam tension, result tension or career worries: talk it out with a listener instead of keeping it all inside.

How can talking help during exam stress?

Many students find that when they say their worries aloud, the thoughts feel less tangled and it becomes easier to plan the next small step. A listener will not judge your marks or compare you with anyone. They are there to hear you out.

Exam stress kaise kam kare: a few things to try today

  • Break the syllabus into small daily targets.
  • Take short breaks and sleep properly, even in the last week.
  • Avoid comparing your preparation with others.
  • Talk to a friend, a family member or a listener about how you feel.

How Bondty works for exam stress

Log in with your mobile number and OTP, top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking, then choose a listener and start a chat or voice call. You pay per minute, and nothing is charged if the request is not accepted. You can also read how Bondty works.

Bondty is for adults aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please talk to a parent, teacher or school counsellor.

Common questions

How can I reduce exam stress?

Small daily targets, regular breaks, enough sleep and talking about your worries help many people. If the stress feels unmanageable or lasts a long time, please speak to a doctor or a counsellor.

Are listeners counsellors?

No. Listeners are independent partners who listen. Bondty is not a medical or therapy service.

What will it cost?

Each listener's rate per minute is shown before you start. 1 coin = ₹1, and you pay ₹0 if the request is not accepted.

Bondty is a platform for conversation only. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees, and Bondty is not a medical, therapy or emergency service. If you feel unsafe or are thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416) right away. 18+ only.
