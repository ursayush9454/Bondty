Talk to strangers online, without sharing your number

Sometimes it is easier to open up to someone who does not know you. On Bondty you can talk to a stranger online over chat, voice call or video call, and your phone number and links stay hidden.

How can you talk to strangers on Bondty?

Tap Random Connect to talk to a verified Listener who is free right now, or browse profiles and pick someone yourself. Every Listener is an independent partner who completes ID and profile verification before taking sessions, so you are talking to a real, verified person.

Why talk to a stranger online?

A stranger has no history with you and no opinion about your family, your job or your past. Many people find it easier to say what is on their mind to someone new: stress at work, exam pressure, loneliness, or a bad day they do not want to explain to friends.

Chat, voice call or video call with a stranger

  • Chat: type at your own pace, with numbers and links hidden.
  • Voice call: talk out loud, with noise suppression for a clearer call.
  • Video call: talk face to face, with auto video blur if you want more privacy.

Is it safe to talk to strangers online?

Bondty is for adults aged 18 and over. Listeners see only your first name, numbers and links stay hidden in chat, every partner ID is verified, and you can report any session in one tap. Read more on our safety page. As with any online conversation, never share bank details, OTPs or other sensitive information.

How to start in 3 steps

  1. Log in with your mobile number and OTP.
  2. Top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking. 1 coin = ₹1.
  3. Choose a listener, or tap Random Connect for whoever is free, and start a chat, voice call or video call.

Common questions

Is it free to talk to strangers on Bondty?

Downloading and browsing are free. You pay per minute only while a conversation is running, from a prepaid wallet. Live Rooms are free.

Is Bondty a dating app?

No. Bondty is a conversation platform for people who want to be heard. It is not a dating app.

Can I stay anonymous?

Listeners see only your first name, you can use any name you like, and numbers and links are hidden in chat.

Bondty is a platform for conversation only. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees, and Bondty is not a medical, therapy or emergency service. If you feel unsafe or are thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416) right away. 18+ only.
