App to vent anonymously: say it all, to someone who listens

Sometimes you do not need advice. You just need to let it out. On Bondty you can vent to a verified listener over chat or voice call, without sharing your phone number.

Why venting to a person helps

Bottling things up makes them louder. When you say a frustrating day, an argument or a worry out loud to someone who is actually listening, it often feels lighter. A listener will not judge you, interrupt you or post about it. They are there to hear you out.

Vent without being judged: how Bondty keeps it private

  • Your phone number stays hidden, and listeners see only your first name
  • You can use any name you like
  • Numbers and links are hidden in chat
  • You can end a session at any time and report any session in one tap

How to vent on Bondty in 3 steps

  1. Log in with your mobile number and OTP.
  2. Top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking. 1 coin = ₹0.80.
  3. Choose a listener, or tap Random Connect, and start a chat or voice call.

What can you vent about?

Anything: a boss who never says thanks, a hard week at college, an argument at home or just a bad day. For specific topics see talk to someone online, work pressure tips and loneliness chat in India.

What it costs

Bondty is free to download. You pay per minute from a prepaid wallet at the rate shown on the listener’s profile, and you pay ₹0 if a request is not accepted. See pricing.

Common questions

Can I vent anonymously on Bondty?

Yes. Your number is hidden, listeners see only your first name, and you can use any name you like. Numbers and links are hidden in chat.

Is venting on Bondty free?

Downloading is free. Conversations are billed per minute at the listener's rate, shown before you start, and you pay ₹0 if the request is not accepted.

Is venting to a listener the same as therapy?

No. Listeners are independent partners who listen. Bondty is not a medical, therapy or emergency service.

Who can use the vent app?

Bondty is for adults aged 18 and over in India.

Bondty is a platform for conversation only. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees, and Bondty is not a medical, therapy or emergency service.
