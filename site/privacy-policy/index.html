Privacy Policy

Bondty

ℹ️ This legal text is available in English only.

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Deific Digital ("Bondty", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use the Bondty User App, Bondty Partner App and our website.

We process personal data in accordance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

This policy applies to both Users and Partners.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

A. Account Information

  • Mobile number used for registration and OTP verification.
  • Name or display name.
  • Gender, where provided.
  • Profile details voluntarily added by users.

B. Partner Information

  • Profile photograph.
  • Biography and profile information.
  • Topics, interests and languages.
  • Government-issued identity documents for verification.
  • Bank account or UPI details required for partner payouts.

Sensitive financial information, such as bank account details, is stored using encryption and restricted access.

C. Transaction Information

  • Wallet recharges.
  • Coin purchases and usage.
  • Virtual gifts and earnings.
  • Withdrawal and payout records.
  • Transaction history.

Payments made through supported payment methods are processed by Razorpay. We do not store users' complete card details or payment credentials.

D. Communication and Session Information

  • User and Partner interaction records.
  • Call start times and durations.
  • Text chat messages.
  • Billing and session history.

We do not record or store voice or video call recordings.

E. Device and Technical Information

  • Device type and model.
  • Operating system.
  • App version.
  • Technical logs.
  • Diagnostic and security information required to operate the Platform and prevent fraud.

F. Camera and Microphone Information

Bondty may request camera and microphone permissions to provide its communication features.

Camera permission may be used for:

  • Enabling video calls between Users and Partners.
  • Capturing or uploading profile photographs, where supported and chosen by the user.

Microphone permission may be used for:

  • Enabling voice calls.
  • Enabling audio communication during video calls.

Camera and microphone access is used for the relevant features when required and permitted by the user.

We do not record voice or video calls.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Verifying mobile numbers through OTP.
  • Verifying Partner identities.
  • Processing payments, wallet recharges and payouts.
  • Connecting Users and Partners for calls and chats.
  • Managing session billing, coins, gifts and earnings.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Handling complaints and disputes.
  • Preventing fraud, misuse and unauthorized access.
  • Improving app functionality and performance.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not sell users' personal information.

3. How Your Information Is Shared

We limit access to personal information according to operational requirements.

User Information

  • Users' mobile numbers are not displayed to other users.
  • Partners may see a User's first name or chosen display name.
  • Personal account information is accessible only to authorized personnel when required.

Partner Information

  • Partner names, profile photographs, topics, languages and ratings may be visible to Users.
  • Government ID documents and payout information are restricted to authorized administrative personnel.
  • Partner financial information is used for verification and payouts.

Chat and Communication Records

Our authorized team may review relevant chat records when necessary to investigate reported incidents, suspicious activity, fraud, safety concerns or legal matters.

4. Legal Disclosure

We may disclose personal information when required:

  • By applicable laws or regulations.
  • By a court order or lawful government request.
  • To investigate fraud or unlawful activity.
  • To protect the rights, safety and security of users, Partners or the Platform.

We disclose only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill the purposes described in this policy and meet applicable legal obligations.

When a user requests account deletion:

  • The associated account will be deleted.
  • Personal profile information will be deleted or anonymized.
  • Associated personal information will be deleted, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
  • Certain transaction, tax, accounting or fraud-prevention records may be retained for the period required by law.

We do not retain personal information longer than necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, except where legally required.

6. Account Deletion

Users and Partners can request deletion of their Bondty accounts.

In-App Account Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion through the account deletion option available within the respective Bondty app.

Website Account Deletion

Users can also submit an account deletion request through our external account deletion webpage:

Account Deletion URL: https://bondty.com/account-deletion/

Users may be asked to provide their registered mobile number and account type to verify the deletion request.

After verification, we will process the request and delete the associated personal information, except information that must be retained under applicable laws or for legitimate legal and security purposes.

Temporary account suspension or deactivation is not a substitute for account deletion.

7. User Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable laws, users may have the right to:

  • Access their personal information.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request deletion of their personal information.
  • Raise privacy-related complaints.
  • Nominate another person to exercise applicable rights in case of death or incapacity.

To exercise these rights, contact us at:

Support Email: support@bondty.com

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect personal information, including:

  • Encryption during data transmission.
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive financial information.
  • Access controls and restricted administrative access.
  • Secure authentication and login tokens.
  • Monitoring and measures to prevent unauthorized access and fraud.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no electronic storage or internet transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

In the event of a personal data breach, we will take appropriate action and provide notifications as required by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Bondty User App and Bondty Partner App are intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 years and above.

We do not knowingly permit individuals under 18 to create or maintain accounts.

If we discover that an account belongs to an individual under 18, we may take appropriate action, including account closure and deletion of associated information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, data practices or applicable laws.

Any material changes will be communicated through the app or other appropriate channels.

The updated Privacy Policy will be published on our website with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

For questions, concerns, complaints or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information, please contact:

Company: Deific Digital

Platform: Bondty User App and Bondty Partner App

Email: hello@deificdigital.com

Support Email: support@bondty.com

Website: https://bondty.com/
