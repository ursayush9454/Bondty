Voice call app for India: talk out loud to a listener

Some things are easier to say than to type. With a voice call on Bondty you can talk out loud to a verified listener, and your phone number stays hidden.

Why choose a voice call?

Your tone and your pauses carry feelings that text cannot. Many people find a voice call more natural when they want to explain a long, tangled situation, or when they simply want to hear a calm voice at the end of the day.

Voice call features on Bondty

  • Noise suppression, so the call stays clear
  • Mute and speaker are one tap away
  • A live timer, so you always know how long you have talked
  • Your number stays hidden, and you can end the call at any time

See all features or the app screens.

How to start a voice call

  1. Log in with your mobile number and OTP.
  2. Top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking. 1 coin = ₹0.80.
  3. Choose a listener and tap voice call, or use Random Voice to connect to a free listener.

You pay per minute. If the listener does not accept, you pay ₹0.

Common questions

Is the voice call free?

Downloading the app is free. Voice calls are billed per minute at the listener's rate, shown before you start.

Will the listener see my phone number?

No. Listeners see only your first name, and you can use any name you like.

What if my balance runs out during the call?

The session ends automatically. You get a warning when your balance is low, and you can top up to keep talking.
