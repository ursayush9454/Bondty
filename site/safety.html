Anonymous chat app India: how your identity stays protected

Bondty works as an anonymous chat app for India. Your phone number is never shown to listeners, listeners see only your first name, and you can use any name you like. Numbers and links are hidden in chat, and on video calls the picture blurs automatically if no face is in frame. Every partner completes ID and profile verification, and you can report any session in one tap. Read how it works in how Bondty works or see talk to someone online.
