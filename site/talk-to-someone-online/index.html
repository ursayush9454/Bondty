Talk to someone online, whenever you need to

Sometimes you do not need advice. You just need someone to hear you out. On Bondty you can talk to someone online over chat, voice call or video call, without sharing your phone number.

What does it mean to talk to someone online on Bondty?

Bondty is an online listener app for India. Instead of posting into a feed or waiting for a reply from friends who are asleep, you can talk to someone online in real time, over chat, voice call or video call. The person on the other side is a verified Listener or Expert who is there to hear you out, not to judge you or give you a lecture.

Who can you talk to on Bondty?

Bondty has verified Listeners and Experts. Each profile shows ratings, languages, topics and the per-minute rate, so you can pick someone you feel comfortable with. Listeners are independent partners who complete ID and profile verification before they take sessions. If you do not want to choose, Random Connect matches you with whoever is free.

Chat, voice call or video call: which one should you pick?

  • Chat suits you if typing feels safer. You can see the typing indicator and a live timer, and you can end whenever you want.
  • Voice call suits you if it is easier to say things than to write them. Noise suppression keeps the call clear, and mute and speaker are one tap away. Read more about the voice call app for India.
  • Video call suits you if you want to talk face to face. If no face is in frame, the video blurs automatically.

How to talk to someone online in 3 steps

  1. Log in with your mobile number and OTP.
  2. Top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking. 1 coin = ₹0.80, and some packs add bonus coins.
  3. Choose a listener, or tap Random Connect for whoever is free, and start a chat, voice call or video call.

What can you talk about?

Anything on your mind. There is no script and no fixed topic. Many people use Bondty for:

  • Exam preparation stress, see our page on exam stress talk for college students and aspirants
  • Work pressure and career worries, or just letting off steam after a long day
  • Loneliness, for example after moving to a new city, see loneliness chat in India
  • Family talks, a tough decision, interview nerves, or a heart-to-heart late at night
  • Venting without being judged, see our app to vent anonymously

What to expect in your first conversation

When a listener accepts your request, the timer starts and the conversation begins. You can say as much or as little as you like, change the topic, or end the session at any time. A listener will not diagnose you or tell you what to do with your life. They are there to listen. If your balance runs low you get a warning, and when it ends the session stops automatically with no extra charges.

Is it free to talk to someone online? How the cost works

Downloading and browsing Bondty are free. Talking is billed per minute from a prepaid wallet, at the rate shown on each listener’s profile before you start. If a listener does not accept your request, you pay ₹0. This is why many people call Bondty a paid listener app: you pay only for the minutes you use, with no subscription. See pricing for details.

Private, and you stay in control

Your number and links stay hidden in chat, and listeners see only your first name, so you can use any name you like. You can report any session in one tap. You can also stop whenever you want, so you are always in control of both the conversation and the cost. Read more about safety on Bondty.

Who is Bondty for, and who is it not for?

Bondty is for adults aged 18 and over in India who want someone to talk to. It is not dating, therapy or an emergency service, and listeners are not doctors or counsellors. If you need medical or mental health care, please see a qualified professional.

Common questions

Is it free to talk to someone online on Bondty?

Downloading and browsing are free. You pay per minute only while a conversation is running, from a prepaid wallet.

Can I stay anonymous?

Listeners see only your first name, you can use any name you like, and numbers and links are hidden in chat.

Is this therapy?

No. Bondty is a conversation platform, not a medical, therapy or emergency service. For medical or mental health care, please see a qualified professional.

How much does it cost to talk to someone online in India?

Each listener sets a per-minute rate that is shown before you start. 1 coin = ₹0.80, you pay only for the minutes you talk, and you pay ₹0 if the request is not accepted.

What happens if my balance runs out during a conversation?

You get a warning when your balance is low. If it runs out, the session ends automatically, and you can top up to keep talking.

Can I talk to someone online without sharing my phone number?

Yes. Your number is hidden, listeners see only your first name, and links and numbers are hidden in chat.

Can I use Bondty if I am under 18?

No. Bondty is only for adults aged 18 and over.
