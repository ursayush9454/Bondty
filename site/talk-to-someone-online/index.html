Talk to someone online, whenever you need to

Sometimes you do not need advice. You just need someone to hear you out. On Bondty you can talk to someone online over chat, voice call or video call, without sharing your phone number.

Who can you talk to on Bondty?

Bondty has verified Listeners and Experts. Each profile shows ratings, languages, topics and the per-minute rate, so you can pick someone you feel comfortable with. Listeners are independent partners who complete ID and profile verification before they take sessions.

How to talk to someone online in 3 steps

  1. Log in with your mobile number and OTP.
  2. Top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking. 1 coin = ₹1.
  3. Choose a listener, or tap Random Connect for whoever is free, and start a chat, voice call or video call.

What can you talk about?

Anything on your mind. Many people use Bondty for:

  • Exam stress, see our page on exam stress talk
  • Work pressure and career worries
  • Loneliness, see loneliness chat in India
  • Family talks, interview tips, or just venting after a long day

Private, and you stay in control of the cost

Your number and links stay hidden in chat, and listeners see only your first name, so you can use any name you like. You pay by the minute from a prepaid wallet. If a listener does not accept your request, you pay ₹0, and you can end a session at any time.

Common questions

Is it free to talk to someone online on Bondty?

Downloading and browsing are free. You pay per minute only while a conversation is running, from a prepaid wallet.

Can I stay anonymous?

Listeners see only your first name, you can use any name you like, and numbers and links are hidden in chat.

Is this therapy?

No. Bondty is a conversation platform, not a medical, therapy or emergency service. For medical or mental health care, please see a qualified professional.

Bondty is a platform for conversation only. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees, and Bondty is not a medical, therapy or emergency service. If you feel unsafe or are thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416) right away. 18+ only.
