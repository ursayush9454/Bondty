About Bondty

What is Bondty? An online listener app for India

Bondty is an online listener app where you can talk to someone online whenever something is on your mind. You choose a verified Listener or Expert and talk over chat, voice call or video call, privately and without judgement. There is nothing to schedule and no phone number to share.

It is made for people who have no one to talk to right now: students dealing with exam stress, working professionals under work pressure, people who feel lonely, or anyone who just wants to vent after a long day. Listeners are independent partners who complete ID and profile verification before they take sessions.

Getting started takes three steps. Log in with your mobile number and OTP, top up your wallet with UPI, card or netbanking (1 coin = ₹1), then pick a listener and start talking. You pay per minute only for the time you talk, and nothing is charged if your request is not accepted.

Your number stays hidden and listeners see only your first name. Bondty is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not dating, therapy or an emergency service. Learn more about safety on Bondty and how it works, or explore talk to someone online, exam stress talk and loneliness chat in India.

FAQ

Bondty FAQ: payments, privacy and conversations

How am I charged?

Each listener’s rate is shown in coins per minute. The timer starts when the conversation starts. A partial minute counts as a full minute. 1 coin = ₹1.

What if the listener doesn’t accept my request?

You aren’t charged. Nothing leaves your wallet until the conversation starts.

What if my balance runs out mid-session?

The session ends automatically. You get a warning when balance is low, and you can top up to keep talking.

Can the listener see my number?

No. Listeners only see your first name, and you can use any name you like instead of your real one.

Is Bondty free to download?

Yes. Downloading and browsing are free. You pay only for conversation time from a prepaid wallet.
