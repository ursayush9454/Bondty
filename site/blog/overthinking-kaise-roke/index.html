Overthinking kaise roke: 6 ways to quiet your mind

Published 27 November 2026 · Bondty team

Overthinking is when the same thought keeps going around and around, whether it is a conversation you replay, a decision you cannot make or a worry about what might happen. It is very common and it is tiring. These six habits can help you break the loop for a while.

1. Notice the loop

Say to yourself, "I am overthinking right now." Seeing the pattern puts a little distance between you and the thought.

2. Write the thought down

Put the worry on paper in one or two sentences. Then write the next small step you can take, even if it is only "think about this again tomorrow at 6 pm".

3. Set a worry time

Give yourself 15 minutes a day to think about your worries. When a worry shows up outside that time, tell it to wait.

4. Change what your body is doing

Walk, wash your face, stretch or step outside. A change of scene often interrupts a thought loop better than trying to out-think it.

5. Ask: what can I control?

Split your worry into things you can act on and things you cannot. Put your energy only into the first list.

6. Talk it through with someone

Saying thoughts out loud often shows how they really sound. If you want a patient ear, you can chat or call a verified listener on Bondty, with your number hidden. A listener will not diagnose or treat you, they simply listen.

If overthinking is constant, keeps you from sleeping or working, or comes with panic or very low mood, please speak to a doctor or a mental health professional.
