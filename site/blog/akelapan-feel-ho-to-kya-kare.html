Akelapan dur karne ke tarike: 7 simple things to try today

Published 17 November 2026 · Bondty team

Everyone feels alone sometimes, even people who are surrounded by others. The feeling is uncomfortable, but it does not mean something is wrong with you. Here are seven small things you can try today.

1. Name the feeling

Say to yourself, "I am feeling lonely right now." Naming a feeling often makes it a little easier to handle.

2. Reach out to one person

Send a message to an old friend or a cousin. It does not need to be deep. "Thinking of you, how are you?" is enough.

3. Step outside

A ten-minute walk, a visit to a nearby tea stall or a park can change your mood more than you expect.

4. Move your body

Light stretching, yoga or a short workout helps release tension, even if you do it for just a few minutes.

5. Go easy on late-night scrolling

Seeing everyone else's highlights can make you feel more alone. Try keeping the phone away for the last half hour before sleep.

6. Build a small routine

Fixed meal times, a short evening walk or a hobby slot give the day a shape, and a day with shape feels less empty.

7. Talk to someone who listens

If there is no one around, you can chat or call a verified listener on Bondty, and your number stays hidden. A listener is not a replacement for friends, family or professional care.

If loneliness lasts for weeks, or it affects your sleep, appetite, work or studies, please speak to a doctor or a mental health professional.
