Breakup ke baad kisse baat kare: who to talk to and how

Published 27 November 2026 · Bondty team

A breakup can leave you with a lot of feelings and no clear idea of where to put them. Talking helps, but it matters who you talk to and how. Here is a simple guide.

1. A close friend who can just listen

Choose the friend who does not rush to fix things or judge. Tell them upfront, "I do not need advice right now, I just need to talk."

2. A family member you trust

A sibling, cousin or parent can offer steady support. If you worry about being judged, you can share only as much as feels comfortable.

3. A journal, for the things you cannot say

Writing out the anger, the memories and the "what ifs" lets you feel them without sending a message you may regret.

4. A listener, when you want a fresh ear

Sometimes it is easier to talk to someone outside your circle. On Bondty you can chat or call a verified listener, use any name you like and keep your number hidden. Conversations are paid per minute. A listener will not take sides, and is not a counsellor or therapist.

5. What to go easy on

Try to avoid late-night messages to your ex, checking their profile again and again, and big decisions in the first few weeks. Small routines such as meals, sleep and a walk help more than they seem to.

Grief after a breakup is normal and it does ease with time. If you feel unable to cope, cannot eat or sleep for days, or feel hopeless, please speak to a doctor or a mental health professional.
