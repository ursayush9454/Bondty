Listener app vs therapy: what is the difference?

Published 27 November 2026 · Bondty team

People often ask whether talking to a listener on an app is the same as therapy. It is not, and knowing the difference helps you choose the right kind of support. This guide is written honestly, including what Bondty does not do.

What a listener app offers

A listener app such as Bondty lets adults chat or call someone who will listen without judging. It is a conversation, available when you want one, and you pay per minute. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees.

What therapy offers

Therapy is delivered by trained and qualified professionals, such as a psychologist or psychiatrist. They can assess a problem, diagnose where appropriate and work with you over time on a plan. A listener cannot do this.

What a listener cannot do

A listener does not diagnose, treat, prescribe medicine or give medical advice. Bondty does not claim that its listeners are licensed therapists, and it is not a crisis or emergency service.

When a listener conversation can help

If you are lonely, stressed about work or exams, going through a breakup or just want to say things out loud, a conversation with someone who listens can make a day feel lighter. See how Bondty works and the pricing before you start.

When to see a professional

If your low mood, anxiety or sleep problems last for weeks, affect daily life, or you are thinking about harming yourself, please contact a doctor or a mental health professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416).

They can work together

Many people use both: a professional for treatment and a friendly conversation on a hard evening. One does not replace the other.

Bondty is a platform for conversation only. Listeners are independent partners, not Bondty employees, and Bondty is not a medical, therapy or emergency service. If you feel unsafe or are thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416) right away. 18+ only.
