Bondty kya hai? India ke liye online listener app

Bondty ek online listener app hai, jahan jab bhi mann mein kuch ho, tum kisi se online baat kar sakte ho. Tum ek verified Listener ya Expert chunte ho aur chat, voice call ya video call par private tareeke se, bina judgement ke baat karte ho. Na koi appointment, na phone number dene ki zaroorat.

Ye un logon ke liye hai jinke paas abhi baat karne ke liye koi nahi hai: exam stress se jujhte students, work pressure mein kaam karne wale professionals, akelapan feel karne wale log, ya koi bhi jo lambe din ke baad bas apne mann ki baat kehna chahta hai. Listeners independent partners hain jo sessions lene se pehle ID aur profile verification complete karte hain. Agar tum strangers se baat karne wala app dhoondh rahe ho, ya stress kaise kam kare ka koi tareeka, to Bondty usi ke liye bana hai.

Shuru karna teen steps ka kaam hai. Mobile number aur OTP se login karo, UPI, card ya netbanking se wallet top up karo (1 coin = ₹1), phir listener chuno aur baat shuru karo. Paise sirf utne minute ke lagte hain jitni baat karo, aur request accept na ho to kuch charge nahi hota.

Tumhara number hidden rehta hai aur listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain. Bondty 18 saal aur usse upar ke logon ke liye hai. Ye dating, therapy ya emergency service nahi hai. Bondty ki safety aur ye kaise kaam karta hai ke baare mein padho, ya dekho kisi se online baat karo, exam stress kaise kam kare aur akelapan dur kare.

FAQ

Bondty FAQ: payment, privacy aur baatcheet ke sawaal

Mujhse kaise charge liya jata hai?

Har listener ka rate coins per minute mein dikhta hai. Timer baatcheet shuru hone par chalu hota hai. Adhura minute poora gina jata hai. 1 coin = ₹1.

Listener request accept na kare to?

Koi charge nahi. Baatcheet shuru hone tak wallet se kuch nahi katta.

Session ke beech balance khatam ho jaye to?

Session apne-aap khatam ho jata hai. Balance kam hone par warning milti hai, top up karke baat jaari rakho.

Kya listener mera number dekh sakta hai?

Nahi. Listeners sirf first name dekhte hain, aur tum asli naam ki jagah koi bhi naam rakh sakte ho.

Kya Bondty download karna free hai?

Haan. Download aur browse karna free hai. Sirf baatcheet ke time ke paise prepaid wallet se lagte hain.
