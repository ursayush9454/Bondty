Raat ko akelapan: lonely raat se nikalne ke 6 tarike

Published 27 November 2026 · Bondty team

Akelapan raat mein aksar zyada tez lagta hai. Din khatam ho gaya, ghar shaant hai aur dimaag ko ghoomne ki jagah mil jaati hai. Agar tum abhi jaag rahe ho aur akela feel kar rahe ho, to tum akele nahi ho. Ye 6 chhoti baatein raat ko thoda halka bana sakti hain.

1. Yaad rakho ki raat mein feelings bade lagte hain

Thaka hua dimaag cheezon ko zyada dark dekhta hai. Jo tension raat 1 baje bahut badi lagti hai, wo subah aksar sambhal jaati hai. Aaj raat sab kuch solve karna zaroori nahi.

2. Kamre ko shaant banao

Light dim karo, phone ko door charging par rakho aur paas mein paani rakho. Shaant jagah body ko bhi slow hone ka signal deti hai.

3. Likh do jo mann mein hai

5 minute likho jo bhi dimaag mein chal raha hai, neat likhne ki koshish ke bina. Baatein kaagaz par aa jaayein to dimaag thoda shaant hota hai.

4. Slow breathing try karo

4 count mein saans andar, 6 count mein bahar. Kuch baar karo. Lambi exhale se body settle hoti hai. Chakkar aaye to ruk jao.

5. Ek chhota sa message bhejo

Agar koi apna jaag raha ho to halka sa message bhejo. "Jaag raha hoon, thoda low lag raha hai, 5 minute baat kar sakte ho?" bilkul theek hai.

6. Aise kisi se baat karo jo sune

Agar koi paas nahi hai to Bondty par verified listener se chat ya call kar sakte ho, aur tumhara number hidden rehta hai. Baat-cheet per minute ke hisaab se paid hai, aur listener dost, family ya professional care ka replacement nahi hai.

Agar neend na aane wali, akeli raatein baar baar aa rahi hain ya kaam, bhookh ya mood par asar pad raha hai, to doctor ya mental health professional se zaroor baat karo.
