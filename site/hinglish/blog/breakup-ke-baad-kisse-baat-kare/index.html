Breakup ke baad kisse baat kare: kis se aur kaise

Published 27 November 2026 · Bondty team

Breakup ke baad bahut saari feelings aati hain aur samajh nahi aata ki unhe kahan rakhein. Baat karne se madad milti hai, lekin ye bhi matter karta hai ki kis se aur kaise baat karte ho. Ye ek simple guide hai.

1. Ek close dost jo bas sun sake

Wo dost chuno jo turant solve karne ya judge karne mein nahi lagta. Shuru mein hi bol do, "Mujhe abhi advice nahi chahiye, bas baat karni hai."

2. Family mein koi jis par bharosa ho

Bhai-behen, cousin ya parents steady support de sakte hain. Judge hone ka darr ho to utna hi share karo jitna comfortable lage.

3. Journal, jo kehna mushkil hai uske liye

Gussa, yaadein aur "agar aisa hota to" likh do. Isse tum feel kar paate ho bina aisa message bhejne ke jiska baad mein pachtawa ho.

4. Listener, jab koi naya sunne wala chahiye

Kabhi apne circle ke bahar ke insaan se baat karna aasaan hota hai. Bondty par verified listener se chat ya call kar sakte ho, koi bhi naam use karo aur number hidden rehta hai. Baat-cheet per minute paid hai. Listener kisi ka side nahi leta, aur wo counsellor ya therapist nahi hai.

5. Kin cheezon se bachna

Raat ko ex ko message karna, baar baar unki profile dekhna aur shuru ke kuch hafton mein bade decisions lena avoid karne ki koshish karo. Khana, neend aur ek chhoti walk jaisi routine tumhari soch se zyada madad karti hai.

Breakup ke baad dukh hona normal hai aur waqt ke saath kam hota hai. Agar sambhalna mushkil lag raha hai, kai din se khana ya neend nahi aa rahi, ya sab kuch hopeless lag raha hai, to doctor ya mental health professional se zaroor baat karo.
