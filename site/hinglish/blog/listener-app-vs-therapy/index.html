Listener app vs therapy: kya fark hai?

Published 27 November 2026 · Bondty team

Log aksar poochte hain ki app par listener se baat karna therapy jaisa hi hai kya. Nahi, ye alag hai, aur fark samajhne se tum sahi support chun paoge. Ye guide honest hai, usme ye bhi hai ki Bondty kya nahi karta.

Listener app kya deta hai

Bondty jaisa listener app adults ko aisa insaan deta hai jisse chat ya call par baat ki ja sake, bina judge hue. Ye ek conversation hai, jab chaho tab, aur per minute paid hai. Listeners independent partners hain, Bondty ke employees nahi.

Therapy kya deti hai

Therapy trained aur qualified professionals deti hain, jaise psychologist ya psychiatrist. Wo problem ko assess kar sakte hain, zaroorat par diagnose kar sakte hain aur waqt ke saath tumhare saath plan par kaam karte hain. Listener ye nahi kar sakta.

Listener kya nahi kar sakta

Listener diagnose, treat, dawa prescribe ya medical advice nahi deta. Bondty ye claim nahi karta ki uske listeners licensed therapists hain, aur ye crisis ya emergency service nahi hai.

Listener se baat kab madad kar sakti hai

Agar akelapan hai, kaam ya exam ka stress hai, breakup chal raha hai ya bas mann ki baat bolni hai, to kisi sunne wale se baat karke din halka lag sakta hai. Shuru karne se pehle Bondty kaise kaam karta hai aur pricing dekh lo.

Professional se kab milo

Agar low mood, anxiety ya neend ki problem hafton se hai, roz ki zindagi par asar daal rahi hai, ya khud ko nuksaan pahunchane ke khayal aa rahe hain, to doctor ya mental health professional se milo. Emergency mein apna local emergency number ya Tele-MANAS (14416) jaisi helpline par call karo.

Dono saath bhi chal sakte hain

Kai log dono use karte hain: treatment ke liye professional, aur mushkil shaam ko ek dostana baat. Ek doosre ki jagah nahi leta.

Bondty sirf baatcheet ka platform hai. Listeners independent partners hain, Bondty ke employees nahi, aur Bondty koi medical, therapy ya emergency service nahi hai.
