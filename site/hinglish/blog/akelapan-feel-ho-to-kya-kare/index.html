Akelapan feel ho to kya kare: aaj try karne layak 7 simple baatein

Published 17 November 2026 · Bondty team

Har kisi ko kabhi na kabhi akelapan feel hota hai, unhe bhi jo logon se ghire rehte hain. Ye ehsaas bechain karta hai, par iska matlab ye nahi ki tumhare andar kuch galat hai. Yahan saat chhoti baatein hain jo tum aaj try kar sakte ho.

1. Ehsaas ko naam do

Khud se kaho, "Mujhe abhi akelapan lag raha hai." Ehsaas ko naam dene se use sambhalna aksar thoda aasan ho jaata hai.

2. Kisi ek insaan tak pahuncho

Kisi purane dost ya cousin ko message bhejo. Use gehra hona zaroori nahi. "Teri yaad aayi, kaise ho?" kaafi hai.

3. Bahar niklo

Das minute ki sair, paas ki chai ki dukaan ya park jaana tumhare mood ko tumhari soch se zyada badal sakta hai.

4. Body ko hilao

Halki stretching, yoga ya chhoti si exercise tension nikalne mein madad karti hai, chahe kuch hi minute karo.

5. Raat mein scrolling kam karo

Sabki acchi acchi photos dekhkar akelapan aur badh sakta hai. Sone se pehle aakhri aadhe ghante phone ko door rakhkar dekho.

6. Ek chhota sa routine banao

Khane ka tay time, shaam ki chhoti sair ya hobby ke liye waqt, din ko ek dhancha dete hain, aur dhanche wala din kam khaali lagta hai.

7. Kisi aise se baat karo jo sunta hai

Agar aaspaas koi nahi hai, to tum Bondty par verified listener se chat ya call kar sakte ho, aur tumhara number hidden rehta hai. Listener doston, family ya professional care ki jagah nahi le sakta.

Agar akelapan hafton tak rahe, ya tumhari neend, bhookh, kaam ya padhai par asar daale, to please doctor ya mental health professional se baat karo.
