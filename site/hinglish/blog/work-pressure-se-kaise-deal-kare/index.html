Work pressure se kaise deal kare: 6 practical tips

Published 17 November 2026 · Bondty team

Deadlines, targets, lambe ghante aur aisa phone jo bajna band hi nahi karta. Work pressure chupchap badhta jaata hai. Ye chhe aadatein tumhe zyada control mein feel karane mein madad kar sakti hain.

1. Jo kuch karna hai, sab likh lo

List bana lene se chinta dimaag se bahar aa jaati hai. Phir un do teen cheezon par nishaan lagao jo aaj sach mein zaroori hain.

2. Ek samay mein ek kaam karo

Ek kaam ke liye 45 se 60 minute tay karo, notifications band rakho, aur phir chhota break lo.

3. Politely boundaries tay karo

Tum keh sakte ho, "Main apna workload dekhkar shaam tak batata hu." Haan kehne se pehle ka ek pause tumhe zaroorat se zyada kaam lene se bachata hai.

4. Real breaks lo

Lunch desk se door khao, paani piyo aur stretch karo. Phone par scroll karna dimaag ke liye real break nahi hai.

5. Din ko ek closing ritual se khatam karo

Kal ke teen zaroori kaam likho, laptop band karo aur office notifications band kar do. Isse dimaag ko signal milta hai ki aaj ka kaam khatam.

6. Baat karke halke ho jao

Kisi bharosemand colleague, dost ya ghar wale se apni feelings baanto. Agar aisa koi nahi hai jisse khulkar baat kar sako, to Bondty par verified listener se chat karke bina judgement ke mann halka kar sakte ho.

Agar tum har waqt thake hue, nirash ya hafton tak kaam se dimaag hata nahi pa rahe, to please doctor ya mental health professional se baat karo. Bondty koi medical ya therapy service nahi hai.
