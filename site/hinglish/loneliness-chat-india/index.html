Akelapan dur kare: India mein listener se chat karo

Bhare hue hostel, office ya sheher mein bhi akelapan feel ho sakta hai. Agar aaj raat baat karne ke liye koi nahi hai, to Bondty par listener chat ya voice call par tumhari baat sun sakta hai.

Akelapan tumhari soch se zyada common hai

Naye sheher mein jaana, nayi job, family se door rehna ya bas ek lambi raat, in sabse akelapan aa sakta hai. Ye ek normal insaani ehsaas hai, aur kisi aise se ise keh dena madad karta hai jo tumhe judge na kare.

Akelapan dur kare: try karne layak chhote kadam

  • Aaj kisi purane dost ko message ya call karo.
  • Thodi der bahar tahal aao.
  • Kisi hobby group, class ya community se jud jao.
  • Jab aaspaas koi na ho, to listener se baat karo.
