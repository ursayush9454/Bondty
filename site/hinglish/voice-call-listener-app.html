India ke liye voice call app: listener se khulkar baat karo

Kuch baatein type karne se bolna aasan hota hai. Bondty par voice call se tum verified listener se khulkar baat kar sakte ho, aur tumhara phone number hidden rehta hai. Typing se zyada call pasand hai? Bondty ko anonymous voice call app ki tarah use karo, kisi naye insaan ya verified listener se baat karne ke liye, noise suppression ke saath aur number hidden rakhkar.

Voice call kyun chune?

Tumhari awaaz ka lehza aur pauses aisi feelings le jaate hain jo text nahi le jaa pata. Kai logon ko lambi, uljhi baat samjhane ke liye, ya din ke ant mein bas ek shaant awaaz sunne ke liye voice call zyada natural lagti hai.

Bondty par voice call ke features

  • Noise suppression, taaki call clear rahe
  • Mute aur speaker bas ek tap door
  • Live timer, taaki tumhe pata rahe kitni der baat hui
  • Tumhara number hidden rehta hai, aur tum kabhi bhi call khatam kar sakte ho

Voice call kaise shuru kare

  1. Mobile number aur OTP se login karo.
  2. UPI, card ya netbanking se wallet top up karo. 1 coin = ₹1.
  3. Listener chuno aur voice call dabao, ya free listener se judne ke liye Random Voice use karo.

Payment per minute hota hai. Listener accept na kare to ₹0 lagta hai.

Aam sawaal

Kya voice call free hai?

App download karna free hai. Voice call ka payment listener ke rate par per minute hota hai, jo shuru karne se pehle dikhta hai.

Kya listener mera phone number dekh sakta hai?

Nahi. Listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain, aur tum koi bhi naam use kar sakte ho.

Agar call ke dauran balance khatam ho jaye to?

Session apne aap khatam ho jaata hai. Balance kam hone par tumhe warning milti hai, aur baat jaari rakhne ke liye tum top up kar sakte ho.
