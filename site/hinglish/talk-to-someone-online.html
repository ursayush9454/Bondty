Kisi se baat karne wala app: jab mann kare, online baat karo

Kabhi kabhi tumhe advice nahi chahiye hoti, bas koi chahiye jo tumhari baat sun le. Bondty par tum phone number bataye bina chat, voice call ya video call par online kisi se baat kar sakte ho.

Bondty par tum kis se baat kar sakte ho?

Bondty par verified Listeners aur Experts hain. Har profile mein ratings, languages, topics aur per minute rate dikhta hai, taaki tum aisa insaan chun sako jiske saath tum comfortable feel karo. Agar tum online dost se baat kare jaisa feel dhoondh rahe ho, jo bina judge kiye sune, to Bondty ke listeners wahi karte hain. Listeners independent partners hain jo sessions lene se pehle ID aur profile verification complete karte hain. Bondty dating app nahi hai.

Online kisi se baat karne ke 3 steps

  1. Mobile number aur OTP se login karo.
  2. UPI, card ya netbanking se wallet top up karo. 1 coin = ₹0.80.
  3. Listener chuno, ya jo free ho usse judne ke liye Random Connect dabao, aur chat, voice call ya video call shuru karo.

Kis baare mein baat kar sakte ho?

Jo bhi mann mein ho. Kai log Bondty ka use inke liye karte hain:

  • Exam stress, dekho: exam stress kaise kam kare
  • Work pressure aur career ki tension
  • Akelapan, dekho: akelapan dur kare
  • Family ki baatein, interview tips, ya lambe din ke baad bas mann halka karna

Private, aur kharcha tumhare control mein

Chat mein tumhara number aur links hidden rehte hain, aur listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain, isliye tum koi bhi naam use kar sakte ho. Payment prepaid wallet se per minute hota hai. Agar listener tumhari request accept nahi karta to ₹0 lagta hai, aur tum kabhi bhi session khatam kar sakte ho.

Kya Bondty par online baat karna free hai?

Download karna aur browse karna free hai. Tum prepaid wallet se sirf tab paise dete ho jab baatcheet chal rahi ho, per minute.

Kya main anonymous reh sakta hu?

Listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain, tum koi bhi naam use kar sakte ho, aur chat mein number aur links hidden rehte hain.

Kya ye therapy hai?

Nahi. Bondty baatcheet ka platform hai, medical, therapy ya emergency service nahi. Medical ya mental health care ke liye kisi qualified professional se milo.
