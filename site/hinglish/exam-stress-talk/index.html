Exam stress kaise kam kare? Kisi aise se baat karo jo sunta hai

Exam ke dino mein sab kuch bhaari lagne lagta hai: syllabus, result, gharwalon ki umeedein. Baat karna padhai ki jagah nahi le sakta, par chinta zor se keh dene se pressure aksar halka lagta hai. Exam ki tension, result ki tension ya career ki chinta: sab andar rakhne ki jagah kisi listener se baat karke mann halka karo.

Exam stress mein baat karne se kya madad milti hai?

Kai students ko lagta hai ki chinta zor se bolne par vichar kam uljhe lagte hain aur agla chhota kadam tay karna aasan ho jaata hai. Listener tumhare marks par judgement nahi karega aur na hi kisi se comparison karega. Wo bas tumhari baat sunne ke liye hai.

Exam stress kaise kam kare: aaj try karne layak kuch baatein

  • Syllabus ko chhote chhote daily targets mein baant lo.
  • Chhote breaks lo aur theek se soo, last week mein bhi.
  • Apni preparation ki dusron se comparison mat karo.
  • Kisi dost, ghar wale ya listener se apni feelings ke baare mein baat karo.

Exam stress ke liye Bondty kaise kaam karta hai

Mobile number aur OTP se login karo, UPI, card ya netbanking se wallet top up karo, phir listener chuno aur chat ya voice call shuru karo. Payment per minute hota hai, aur request accept na ho to kuch charge nahi hota. Tum Bondty kaise kaam karta hai bhi padh sakte ho.

Bondty 18 saal aur usse upar ke logon ke liye hai. Agar tumhari age 18 se kam hai, to please parents, teacher ya school counsellor se baat karo.

Exam stress kaise kam kare?

Chhote daily targets, regular breaks, enough neend aur chintaon par baat karna kai logon ki madad karta hai. Agar stress sambhal se bahar lage ya lambe time tak rahe, to please doctor ya counsellor se baat karo.

Kya listeners counsellor hain?

Nahi. Listeners independent partners hain jo sunte hain. Bondty koi medical ya therapy service nahi hai.

Isme kitna kharcha aayega?

Har listener ka per minute rate shuru karne se pehle dikhta hai. 1 coin = ₹1, aur request accept na ho to ₹0 lagta hai.

Bondty sirf baatcheet ka platform hai. Listeners independent partners hain, Bondty ke employees nahi, aur Bondty koi medical, therapy ya emergency service nahi hai. Agar tum unsafe feel kar rahe ho ya khud ko nuksaan pahunchane ka khayal aa raha hai, to turant apni local emergency service ya Tele-MANAS (14416) jaisi helpline se contact karo. Sirf 18+.
