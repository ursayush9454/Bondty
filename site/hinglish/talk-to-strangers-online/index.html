Strangers se baat karne wala app: bina number diye anjaan se baat karo

Kabhi kabhi unse mann ki baat kehna aasan hota hai jo tumhe jaante hi nahi. Bondty par tum chat, voice call ya video call par kisi anjaan se baat kar sakte ho, aur tumhara number aur links hidden rehte hain.

Bondty par strangers se kaise baat karte hain?

Random Connect dabao to jo verified Listener abhi free hai usse baat shuru ho jaati hai, ya profiles dekhkar khud kisi ko chuno. Har Listener independent partner hai jo sessions lene se pehle ID aur profile verification complete karta hai, isliye tum ek real, verified insaan se baat karte ho. Agar tum online dost se baat karne jaisa feel dhoondh rahe ho, jo bina judge kiye sune, to Bondty ke listeners wahi karte hain.

Anjaan insaan se baat kyun karein?

Anjaan insaan ka tumhare saath koi past nahi hota aur tumhari family, job ya zindagi par koi opinion bhi nahi. Kai log naye insaan se apni baat kehna aasan maante hain: work stress, exam pressure, akelapan, ya aisa bura din jo dosto ko samjhana nahi chahte.

Strangers se chat, voice call ya video call

  • Chat: apni speed se type karo, number aur links hidden rehte hain.
  • Voice call: khulkar baat karo, noise suppression se call clear rehti hai.
  • Video call: aamne-saamne baat karo, aur chaho to auto video blur ke saath.

Kya strangers se online baat karna safe hai?

Bondty sirf 18+ adults ke liye hai. Listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain, chat mein number aur links hidden rehte hain, har partner ID verified hai, aur tum ek tap mein kisi bhi session ko report kar sakte ho. Zyada jaanne ke liye safety page dekho. Kisi bhi online baatcheet ki tarah, bank details, OTP ya sensitive information kabhi share mat karo.

Shuru karne ke 3 steps

  1. Mobile number aur OTP se login karo.
  2. UPI, card ya netbanking se wallet top up karo. 1 coin = ₹1.
  3. Listener chuno, ya jo free ho usse judne ke liye Random Connect dabao, aur chat, voice call ya video call shuru karo.
