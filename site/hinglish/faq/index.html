FAQ

Payments aur baatcheet ke baare mein.

Mujhse kaise charge liya jata hai?

Har listener ka rate coins per minute mein dikhta hai. Timer baatcheet shuru hone par chalu hota hai. Adhura minute poora gina jata hai. 1 coin = ₹1.

Listener request accept na kare to?

Koi charge nahi. Baatcheet shuru hone tak wallet se kuch nahi katta.

Session ke beech balance khatam ho jaye to?

Session apne-aap khatam ho jata hai. Balance kam hone par warning milti hai, top up karke baat jaari rakho.

Kya listener mera number dekh sakta hai?

Nahi. Listeners sirf first name dekhte hain, aur tum asli naam ki jagah koi bhi naam rakh sakte ho.

Kya Bondty download karna free hai?

Haan. Download aur browse karna free hai. Sirf baatcheet ke time ke paise prepaid wallet se lagte hain.

Bondty safe hai?

Bondty safety ko sabse pehle rakhkar banaya gaya hai. Chat mein number aur links hidden rehte hain, listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain, har partner ID aur profile verification se guzarta hai, aur tum ek tap mein kisi bhi session ko report kar sakte ho. Platform sirf 18 saal aur usse upar ke logon ke liye hai. Safety page par aur padho.

Listeners therapist ya doctor hain?

Bondty baatcheet ka platform hai, koi medical, therapy ya emergency service nahi. Listeners independent partners hain, Bondty ke employees nahi. Medical ya mental health care ke liye kisi qualified doctor ya therapist se milo, aur emergency mein apni local emergency service se contact karo.

UPI se wallet recharge kaise kare?

Mobile number aur OTP se login karo, phir wallet top up karo. Razorpay ke through UPI, card ya netbanking se payment kar sakte ho. 1 coin = ₹1, aur kuch packs mein bonus coins milte hain, jo payment se pehle app mein dikhte hain. Details ke liye pricing dekho.

Refund kaise milega?

Agar paise kat gaye lekin coins nahi jude, ya ek hi recharge ke liye do baar paise kate, to amount tumhare original payment method mein wapas aata hai, aam taur par 5 se 7 working days mein. Use na hue purchased coins ka refund recharge ke 7 din ke andar hello@deificdigital.com ya support@bondty.com par email karke ya app ke Help and Support se maanga ja sakta hai. Bonus coins refundable nahi hain. Poori details Refund and Cancellation policy mein hain.

Bondty kaun use kar sakta hai?

Bondty 18 saal aur usse upar ke logon ke liye hai: college students, working professionals aur koi bhi jo kisi se baat karna chahta hai. Agar tumhari age 18 se kam hai, to please parents, teacher ya school counsellor se baat karo.

Kya Bondty dating app hai?

Nahi. Bondty dating service nahi hai. Ye verified listeners se baatcheet ka platform hai, aur ye medical ya therapy services bhi nahi deta.

Kya main Hindi ya Hinglish mein baat kar sakta hu?

Har listener ki profile mein unki languages, ratings, topics aur per minute rate dikhta hai. Shuru karne se pehle profile dekh lo, taaki tum aisa listener chun sako jo tumhari tarah baat karta ho.

Bondty par kis baare mein baat kar sakte hain?

Jo bhi mann mein ho: exam stress, work pressure, akelapan, family ki baatein, interview tips ya lambe din ke baad bas mann halka karna. Listeners bina judgement ke sunne ke liye hain. Aur jaanne ke liye kisi se online baat karo dekho.

Main listener kaise ban sakta hu?

Bondty Partner app lo, ID aur profile verification complete karo, online jao aur chat, voice ya video requests accept karo. Kisi ki madad karne ke har minute ke liye tum kamate ho aur apni earnings app se withdraw kar sakte ho. Partner page dekho.

Baat karne ke liye ready?

Bondty download karo aur kisi aise se baat karo jo sunta hai.
