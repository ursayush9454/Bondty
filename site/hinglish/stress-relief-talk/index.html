Stress hai? Kisi aise se baat karo jo sunta hai

Stress akele uthane par badhta hai. Kisi aise se bolkar kehna jo sunta hai, use halka aur saaf mehsoos kara sakta hai. Bondty par tum chat, voice call ya video call par private tareeke se stress ke baare mein baat kar sakte ho.

Stress mein baat karne se kya madad milti hai?

Chintaon ko shabd dene se samajhna aasan hota hai ki kya zaroori hai aur kya sirf shor. Jab koi bina toke aur bina judge kiye sunta hai, to problem aksar chhoti aur sambhalne layak lagti hai. Baat karna koi ilaaj nahi hai, lekin kai log baad mein halka mehsoos karte hain.

Stress kaise kam kare: aaj try karne layak kuch baatein

  1. Jo cheezein stress de rahi hain unhe likh lo, phir unmein se wo ek chuno jis par aaj kaam ho sakta hai.
  2. Kuch minute saans dheemi karo: 4 ginti mein saans andar lo, 6 ginti mein bahar chhodo.
  3. Screen se door 10 minute bahar tehlo.
  4. Kaamon ke beech kudne ki jagah ek samay mein ek kaam karo.
  5. Raat ko scrolling kam rakho, taaki dimaag shaant ho sake.
  6. Kisi aise se baat karo jo sunta hai.

Aam sawaal

Kya listener se baat karne se stress theek ho jayega?

Nahi. Listener tumhari baat sunta hai; wo doctor ya therapist nahi hai. Agar stress hafton se tumhari neend, bhook ya daily life ko affect kar raha hai, to kisi qualified doctor ya mental health professional se milo.

Kya ye stress counselling ya therapy hai?

Nahi. Bondty baatcheet ka platform hai, medical, therapy ya emergency service nahi.

Kya ye private hai?

Chat mein tumhara number aur links hidden rehte hain aur listeners sirf tumhara first name dekhte hain. Payment prepaid wallet se per minute hota hai, aur tum kabhi bhi session khatam kar sakte ho.

Bondty sirf baatcheet ka platform hai. Listeners independent partners hain, Bondty ke employees nahi, aur Bondty koi medical, therapy ya emergency service nahi hai. Agar tum unsafe feel kar rahe ho ya khud ko nuksaan pahunchane ka khayal aa raha hai, to turant apni local emergency service ya Tele-MANAS (14416) jaisi helpline se contact karo. Sirf 18+.
