Competitive exam ka stress? Kisi aise se baat karo jo sunta hai

JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT, SSC ya college exam ki taiyari mein sab kuch bhaari lagne lagta hai: syllabus, attempts, result, gharwalon ki umeedein. Baat karna padhai ki jagah nahi le sakta, par chinta zor se keh dene se pressure aksar halka lagta hai.

Ye page kiske liye hai?

Ye page college students aur 18 saal ya usse upar ke competitive-exam aspirants ke liye hai, jaise JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT, SSC, banking ya state exams ki taiyari karne wale. Bondty sirf adults ke liye hai.

Exam ki taiyari mein baat karne se kya madad milti hai?

Kai aspirants ko lagta hai ki chinta zor se bolne par vichar kam uljhe lagte hain aur agla chhota kadam tay karna aasan ho jaata hai. Listener tumhare marks par judgement nahi karega aur na hi kisi se comparison karega. Wo bas tumhari baat sunne ke liye hai.

Exam stress kaise kam kare: aaj try karne layak kuch baatein

  • Syllabus ko chhote chhote daily targets mein baant lo.
  • Chhote breaks lo aur theek se soo, last week mein bhi.
  • Apni preparation ki dusron se comparison mat karo.
  • Kisi dost, ghar wale ya listener se apni feelings ke baare mein baat karo.

Akele taiyari kar rahe ho? Aspirant ka akelapan real hai

Lambe self-study ke ghante, naya sheher aur dosto se kam milna aspirants ko akela feel karwa sakta hai. Agar aaj raat koi baat karne wala nahi hai, to ek listener tumhari baat sun sakta hai. Akelapan dur kare page bhi dekho.
